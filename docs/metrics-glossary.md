# 📖 Metrics Glossary — Beyond Hire Rate

---

## 🔵 Funnel Metrics

### Funnel Conversion Rate
**Definition:** The percentage of candidates who advance from one hiring stage to the next.
**Formula:** `(Candidates in Stage N+1 / Candidates in Stage N) × 100`
**Why it matters:** Identifies where candidates are dropping off — screening, interview, or offer stage?

---

## 🟢 Sourcing Metrics

### Source Quality Index
**Definition:** A composite score measuring the quality of hires from each sourcing channel, not just the quantity.
**Components:** Offer acceptance rate + 90-day retention + performance score per source
**Why it matters:** A channel that sends 100 applicants but produces only 1 retained hire is not a good source.

### Source Effectiveness Rate
**Definition:** The percentage of hires from a given source who successfully pass probation.
**Formula:** `(Hires Retained at 90 Days from Source X / Total Hires from Source X) × 100`

---

## 🟡 Speed Metrics

### Time-to-Hire
**Definition:** Number of days between a job opening being posted and an offer being accepted.
**Formula:** `Offer Acceptance Date − Job Opening Date`
**Why it matters:** Long time-to-hire increases the risk of losing top candidates to competitors.

### Time-to-Productivity
**Definition:** Number of days until a new hire reaches full independent performance in their role.
**Measurement:** Based on manager assessment at 30/60/90-day check-ins
**Why it matters:** A fast hire who takes 6 months to become productive may cost more than a slower hire who ramps in 6 weeks.

---

## 🟠 Decision Quality Metrics

### Offer Acceptance Rate
**Definition:** The percentage of job offers that are accepted by candidates.
**Formula:** `(Offers Accepted / Offers Extended) × 100`
**Why it matters:** A low rate signals issues with compensation, employer brand, or candidate experience.

### Quality of Hire Score
**Definition:** A composite score measuring how effective a hiring decision was, post-hire.
**Components:**
- Performance rating at first formal review (weighted 40%)
- Manager satisfaction at 90 days (weighted 30%)
- Retention at 90 days (weighted 30%)

**Formula:** `(Performance × 0.4) + (Manager Score × 0.3) + (Retention Binary × 0.3)`

---

## 🔴 Cost Metrics

### Cost of a Bad Hire
**Definition:** The estimated total financial cost when a hire does not work out.
**Components:**
- Recruitment cost (ads, agency fees, interviewer time)
- Onboarding cost (training, equipment, admin)
- Lost productivity during ramp-up
- Replacement cost if the hire leaves

**Why it matters:** A bad hire can cost between 30%–150% of their annual salary.
